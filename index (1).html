Questions

Before you message us.

What do I need to get started?

Your business name, a short description of what you sell, and any logo or colours you already use. If you have none of these, we start from the brief.

Is the video really made by AI?

We use AI tools for visuals, voice and editing steps. A person plans, directs and checks every video before it reaches you. We tell you which parts are AI-generated so you can label them where a platform asks.

Which file formats will I receive?

Designs come as PNG, JPG and PDF, with SVG for logos. Videos come as MP4 in 16:9 and 9:16, and ads in the sizes you need. Websites go live on your own domain.

Will YouTube automation make me money?

We teach the workflow, the tools and the channel strategy. Results depend on your niche, your consistency and the quality of your videos, so we do not promise views or income.

Do you work with clients outside Minna?

Yes. Most of the process runs on WhatsApp and email, so location is not a barrier.

Use the form below or message us on WhatsApp with what you need. We reply with a plan and a price.
