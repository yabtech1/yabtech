How do I start a project with the studio? +

Click any WhatsApp button on this site or message +234 814 7017 603 with what you want to build, who it is for, and your target deadline. We reply promptly with clarifying questions or a clear project plan.

Our physical studio is based at Falali Plaza, around Mobil, Minna, Niger State, Nigeria, and we collaborate with creators and businesses worldwide.
